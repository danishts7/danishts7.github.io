Amazon FBA Fee Changes 2027 (US): Key Dates and What to Do

Key dates and 3 steps for US store sellers.

By Danish · Danishts, Karachi · 21 November 2026

Amazon announced changes to FBA fees in the US store in November 2026. If you sell on Amazon, or plan to launch a product in 2027, two dates matter. This post explains them in simple words. Always check Seller Central for the exact fees on your own products.

15 January 2027: fulfillment fees change

  • Most FBA fulfillment fees change on this date.
  • According to Amazon, the average increase is about $0.19 per unit. The real change depends on the size and price of each product.

1 February 2027: storage fees change

  • Storage fees move to one age-based monthly fee.
  • Fast-selling stock should cost the same or less, per Amazon.
  • Older stock that stays in the warehouse costs more.

What stays the same

The fuel and logistics surcharge stays at 3.5%.

What to do

  1. Check your ASINs in Seller Central. See which of your products are affected and by how much.
  2. Recalculate your margin with the new fees. Use Amazon's own fee tools, such as the Revenue Calculator, to test each product.
  3. Plan your inventory so it does not sit long. Under an age-based fee, slow stock becomes more expensive each month.
Small example (made-up numbers): an extra $0.19 on each unit means about $190 more fulfillment cost on 1,000 units. If your margin is thin, even a small change like this can matter, so check it before you order stock.

If you are planning a launch in 2027, include the new fees in your numbers from the start. See how this fits with the steps from product research to launch and with your PPC budget plan.

Bottom line: launching in 2027? Recalculate your profit with the new fees first.

Common questions

When do the new Amazon FBA fees start in 2027?

Most FBA fulfillment fees change on 15 January 2027, and storage fees change on 1 February 2027. These dates are for the US store, based on Amazon's November 2026 announcement.

How much will FBA fulfillment fees increase?

According to Amazon, the average increase is about $0.19 per unit. The real change varies with the size and price of your product, so check your own ASINs in Seller Central.

What is the new age-based storage fee?

From 1 February 2027, storage fees move to one age-based monthly fee. Fast-selling stock should cost the same or less, and older stock costs more.

Does the fuel and logistics surcharge change?

No. The surcharge stays at 3.5%.

Should I still launch a new product in 2027?

Possibly, but recalculate your margin with the new fees first. Include fulfillment, storage and your PPC budget in the same plan.
