My Journey Wasn't Traditional — But It Was Real

I grew up in Karachi in a hardworking family where financial resources were limited.

My interest in technology began with traditional signboard painting. Around 2002–2003, a cousin introduced me to computers at a local internet café, where I first experienced Windows 98. I didn't have a computer of my own, but I was fascinated by what technology could do.

In 2007, I joined a Karachi-based company as an office assistant. My daily responsibilities included cleaning, preparing tea and supporting office staff. Whenever I found spare time, especially after office hours, I explored an available computer.

Gradually, through experimentation, tutorials and guidance from people around me, I learned Windows, software installation, basic hardware troubleshooting, Adobe Photoshop, design software and networking.

My job title didn't reflect my interest in technology, but I kept learning.

Office Network Optimization — Karachi

Environment

Approximately 25–28 staff members, around 19 desktop PCs, and additional laptops.

Challenge

An existing office network experienced inconsistent internet speeds, recurring connectivity problems, and uneven bandwidth distribution.

My contribution

  • Improved existing router configurations
  • Configured internet load balancing
  • Implemented approximately 6 Mbps per-user bandwidth limits
  • Organized fixed IP configurations
  • Improved wired LAN connectivity
  • Replaced problematic cable connectors
  • Documented network equipment and settings
  • Recommended cost-effective improvements

Outcome

Reported improvements in network stability and fewer recurring connectivity complaints.

Please note: This was optimization of an existing network, not a complete network installation from scratch.

The Story Behind the Name Danishts

Every name has a story, and Danishts is no different.

Back in 2013, I was the person friends and family often approached whenever they had problems with their computers, mobile phones, or other electronic devices. I enjoyed troubleshooting, sharing what I knew, and helping people find practical solutions.

My cousin and a close friend named Adil would often jokingly refer to me as the guy who always had a solution for technology problems. That simple nickname gave me an idea.

When I decided to build my own website in 2014, I chose the name Danish Technology Solutions, shortened to Danishts.com. At the time, it was simply a personal project driven by curiosity and my love for technology.

Over the years, my skills expanded from computers and networking into Amazon E-commerce and digital services. Today, I've given the name a broader meaning:
