Danish — Amazon FBA Consultant & IT Networking Professional

I'm Danish, founder of Danishts, based in Karachi, Pakistan. I'm a self-taught IT and networking professional with hands-on experience designing complete office network infrastructures — from small office LAN setups to enterprise-grade dual-WAN networks with load balancing, CCTV integration, and centralized data backup systems.

Alongside IT, I support Amazon FBA Private Label sellers through the product-to-launch process. My work includes product hunting and niche research, competitor analysis and tracking, keyword research, supplier sourcing, product cost and profit calculations, launch budget planning, listing content and SEO optimization, and PPC budget forecasting. The goal is to help sellers make informed decisions with a clear, practical launch roadmap.

For IT projects, I work on office network design, LAN and dual-WAN setups, connectivity troubleshooting, file sharing, backups and remote technical support. I serve clients on-site in Karachi and remotely across Pakistan and worldwide, including the UAE and USA.
