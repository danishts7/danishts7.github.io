Buying a Phone? Do Not Look at RAM Only: 6 Things to Check

Why some phones hang even with big RAM.

By Danish · Danishts, Karachi · 22 November 2026

Many people buy a phone by looking at RAM and storage only. Then the phone still hangs. The reason is that RAM is just one part of the story. Here are 6 things to check before you buy.

6 things to check

  1. RAM and storage are not the whole story. A weak processor can still make a phone hang.
  2. Check the processor: its tier (entry, mid-range or flagship) and its generation. A higher tier and a newer generation are usually faster.
  3. Check the storage type too. Faster storage (look for UFS) helps apps open quickly. Then confirm on the brand's official website.
  4. Check update support: how many years of updates will the brand give?
  5. Camera: more megapixels does not always mean a better camera. Check sample photos and reviews.
  6. Tell an AI tool (ChatGPT, Gemini or Claude) your daily use and budget, compare options, then confirm on the brand's official website.

Where to check

  • The brand's official website.
  • GSMArena for specs.
  • Geekbench for benchmarks.
Buy for how you use it, not for the biggest numbers. Models and specs change, so always check the latest official details.

Common questions

Does more RAM make a phone faster?

Not always. RAM and storage are not the whole story. A weak processor can still make a phone hang, even with big RAM.

What should I check in a phone processor?

Check its tier (entry, mid-range or flagship) and its generation. A higher tier and a newer generation are usually faster.

What is UFS storage?

UFS is a faster type of phone storage that helps apps open quickly. Look for it in the specs and confirm on the brand's official website.

Do more megapixels mean a better camera?

Not always. Check sample photos and reviews instead of trusting the megapixel number alone.

Where can I check phone specs and speed?

Use the brand's official website, GSMArena for specs and Geekbench for benchmarks. Models and specs change, so check the latest details.
