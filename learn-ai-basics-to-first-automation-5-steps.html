Want to Learn AI? From AI Basics to Your First Automation

5 steps: start small, practice daily, automate smartly.

By Danish · Danishts, Karachi · 22 November 2026

If you want to learn AI but do not know where to start, follow these 5 steps. They go from the basics to your first automation. Start small, practice daily and automate smartly.

5 steps

  1. Understand AI. Learn what AI is and how it helps in daily work.
  2. Explore AI tools. Start with ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini.
  3. Practice prompting. Ask AI questions in Urdu or English. Learn to give clear instructions.
  4. Build a WhatsApp AI agent. Create an AI assistant using your business FAQs and connect it to WhatsApp Business.
  5. Learn automation. Explore n8n and Make to connect apps and automate repetitive tasks.
Start small. Practice daily. Automate smartly. Begin with free AI tools before exploring advanced automation.

Two rules to remember

  • Always review AI responses. AI can make mistakes.
  • Protect customer information. Read what your staff should never paste into AI tools.

For a path that starts from computer basics, read how to learn AI from zero: a 6-step path. For steps 4 and 5 in more detail, see AI automation basics for WhatsApp and email.

Common questions

How do I start learning AI?

Start by understanding what AI is, then try ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini, and practice giving clear instructions every day.

Can I ask AI questions in Urdu?

Yes. You can ask AI questions in Urdu or English. The key is to give clear instructions.

What is a WhatsApp AI agent?

It is an AI assistant built from your business FAQs and connected to WhatsApp Business, so it can give first answers to common questions.

What are n8n and Make?

They are automation tools that connect apps so repetitive tasks can run on their own. Explore them after you are comfortable with AI tools.

Do I need to pay to start?

No. Begin with free AI tools before exploring advanced automation. Plans and limits change, so check each tool.
