AI Automation for Your Business: WhatsApp and Email Basics

Let AI handle the repeat work in your business.

By Danish · Danishts, Karachi · 21 November 2026

Many businesses spend hours every day on the same small tasks: answering the same questions, saving orders, sorting email and sending reminders. Tools like Make and n8n can connect your apps, so these tasks run on their own and AI can read and write for you. Here are the basics.

What to automate on WhatsApp

  • First answers to common questions.
  • Orders and leads saved to a sheet.
  • Reminders and follow-ups.

What to automate in email

  • Sort and label incoming mail.
  • Draft replies for you to review.
  • Summaries and daily digests.

3 rules to follow

  1. Start with one small task. Get it working before you add more.
  2. Check important messages yourself. AI can make mistakes.
  3. Follow platform rules and protect customer data. Read the terms of each platform you connect.
Automate the repeat work. Keep the human touch. Tools and rules change, so check each platform's terms before you build.

Common questions

What is business automation?

It means using tools to run repeat tasks on their own, such as sorting email or saving orders to a sheet, so you spend less time on them.

What are Make and n8n?

They are tools that connect your apps, so one app can trigger an action in another without manual work.

What should I automate first?

Pick one small, repeat task, such as sorting incoming email or saving new leads to a sheet. Get it working before you add more.

Can AI reply to customers on its own?

It can draft first answers, but check important messages yourself because AI can make mistakes.

Is it safe to connect customer data to automation tools?

Be careful. Follow each platform's rules, protect customer data and share only what the task needs.
