Terms & Refund Policy

Please read this before we start working together. It explains how a project is agreed, paid for and, in some cases, refunded.

1. About us

Danishts is a business run by Danish in Karachi, Pakistan. It offers IT networking services and Amazon Private Label and Virtual Assistant support, on-site in Karachi and remotely.

2. Services and contract

Before work starts, the scope, timeline, deliverables and payment terms are written in a contract. Questions and first discussions can happen on WhatsApp (+92 321 3434423), but the final contract and payment terms are not agreed on WhatsApp.

3. How the contract works

  • For remote work, or whenever suitable, the contract is sent by email as a PDF.
  • The client reads it and replies by email to say they agree.
  • If the client prefers, the contract can also be printed and signed on paper.
  • Refund terms are part of the contract.

4. Payments

Payment terms are written in the contract, as a hard copy or a soft copy (for example by email). No work starts and no payment is taken before the client has seen the contract.

5. Refund policy

We do accept refunds, but only in certain cases. It is not true that everything is refundable, and it is not true that nothing is refundable.

  • Whether a refund is possible depends on the contract and on the project: what the work is, how much has already been done and what was agreed.
  • The refund terms for each project are written in the contract and told to the client before work starts and before any payment is made.
  • If a problem comes up, contact us on WhatsApp and we will look at it fairly against what was agreed in the contract.
  • Orders placed through Fiverr follow Fiverr's own rules and policies.

6. Client responsibilities

The client gives accurate information and the access the work needs (for example accounts, network access or documents). Delays in providing these can delay the work.

7. No guaranteed results

Amazon sales, rankings, advertising results and approvals depend on the market and on Amazon's own rules. Danishts does not promise any specific sales, profit or ranking.

8. Blog and website content

Blog posts are general guidance, not a replacement for a proper check of your own setup. Please check your own network or account before making changes to anything live.

9. Changes to these terms

These terms may be updated from time to time. The latest version is always on this page.
