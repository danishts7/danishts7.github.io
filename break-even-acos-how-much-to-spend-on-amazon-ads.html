Break-Even ACoS: How Much Can You Spend on Amazon Ads?

Know your limit before you launch.

By Danish · Danishts, Karachi · 22 November 2026

Before you spend a single dollar on Amazon ads, you should know your limit. That limit is called break-even ACoS. If your ACoS goes above it, you lose money on every sale.

The formula

Break-even ACoS = your profit margin before ads.

To find it, take your price, subtract all your costs before ads (product, shipping, Amazon fees and so on), and divide the profit that is left by the price.

Example (made-up numbers)

  • Price: $20
  • Costs before ads: $14
  • Profit before ads: $6

Margin = $6 / $20 = 30%, so break-even ACoS = 30%.

What happens at different ACoS levels

  • ACoS 25%: ads cost $5 per sale, so profit is $1.
  • ACoS 30%: ads cost $6 per sale, so you break even.
  • ACoS 40%: ads cost $8 per sale, so you lose $2.

3 tips

  1. Set your target ACoS below break-even.
  2. At launch you may go a little above on purpose, but only with a plan and a daily cap.
  3. Fees or costs changed? Recalculate. See the FBA fee changes coming in 2027.

Common questions

What is break-even ACoS?

It is the highest ACoS you can run without losing money. It equals your profit margin before ads. If your ACoS is above it, you lose money on each sale.

How do I calculate break-even ACoS?

Subtract your costs before ads (product, shipping, Amazon fees and others) from your price, then divide the remaining profit by your price. In the example, $6 profit on a $20 price is 30%.

What is a good ACoS on Amazon?

It depends on your margin. A good target ACoS is below your break-even ACoS, so every sale still leaves some profit.

Can I run ads above break-even ACoS at launch?

You may go a little above on purpose to gather data and reviews, but only with a clear plan and a daily budget cap.

When should I recalculate my break-even ACoS?

Whenever your fees, price or costs change. Even a small change in fees can move your break-even number.
