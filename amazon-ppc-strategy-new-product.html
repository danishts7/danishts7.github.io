Amazon PPC Strategy for a New Product: Manual vs Automatic

Start simple, learn fast, keep what works.

By Danish · Danishts, Karachi · 22 November 2026

A new Amazon product has no data yet, so your first PPC campaigns are really about learning. The goal is simple: spend to learn, not to bleed. Here is a clear way to start.

Start with the right campaign type

  • Have keywords? Run a Manual campaign on them. Set a budget that suits you.
  • No keywords yet? Run an Automatic campaign to discover them.

4 steps after you start

  1. Found search terms that sell? Add them to a Manual campaign.
  2. Run Manual for at least 1 week for a first look, and judge it with enough clicks.
  3. Every week: pause keywords that do not work, lower bids on weak ones, keep the winners, and add useless search terms as negatives.
  4. Budget allows? Add video ads too. Amazon has a free AI video generator (check your account), or you can make your own.
Spend to learn, not to bleed. No result is guaranteed. Check what you are eligible for in your Amazon Ads account.

Keep your numbers in view

Before you run ads, know your budget, bid and ACoS. Read why every launch needs a PPC plan, why PPC feels like blind spending and ACoS vs TACoS. Good keywords also come from a strong listing, see the listing keyword plan.

Common questions

Should I start with Manual or Automatic campaigns?

If you already have keywords, run a Manual campaign on them. If you do not have keywords yet, run an Automatic campaign to discover them.

How long should I run a Manual campaign before judging it?

Run it for at least 1 week for a first look, and judge it only with enough clicks.

What should I do every week in Amazon PPC?

Pause keywords that do not work, lower bids on weak ones, keep the winners and add useless search terms as negatives.

What are negative keywords?

They are search terms you tell Amazon not to show your ad for, so you stop paying for clicks that will not sell.

Should I use video ads for a new product?

If your budget allows, yes. Amazon offers a free AI video generator for some accounts (check yours), or you can make your own video. No result is guaranteed.
