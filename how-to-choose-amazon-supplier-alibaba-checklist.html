How to Choose an Amazon Supplier: Alibaba Checklist Before You Pay

Where to look and how to check a supplier.

By Danish · Danishts, Karachi · 22 November 2026

Paying a supplier you do not know is one of the biggest risks in Amazon Private Label. A few simple checks before you pay can protect your money. Here is where to look and what to check.

Where to look for suppliers

  • Alibaba.com: the most popular starting point.
  • Global Sources
  • Made-in-China.com

Our 4-point checklist

  1. Time on the platform: prefer a supplier with 3 or more years.
  2. Verified supplier badge: check that the supplier is verified.
  3. Trade Assurance on your order: make sure your order is covered.
  4. Chat, order and payment on the platform: keep the whole deal inside the platform.

Supplier only 1 to 2 years on the platform?

It is still possible, but make sure the supplier is Verified and your order has Trade Assurance. Order samples first and start with a small order.

Keep these on the platform: payment, compliance, quality and refund talks. Do not move them to WhatsApp, WeChat or email, because then the platform cannot help you if something goes wrong.

These rules are for the start, until you trust the supplier. After 2 to 3 successful orders, you can relax them. Platform protection has terms, so read them. It is not a guarantee.

After the checklist

Talk to more than one supplier and compare. See how to talk to 5 to 8 suppliers, and keep your budget small until the product is proven, as in what to sell on Amazon by budget.

Common questions

Which sites can I use to find a supplier for Amazon FBA?

Alibaba.com is the most popular starting point. Global Sources and Made-in-China.com are two other places to look.

How do I check if an Alibaba supplier is real?

Look at how long the supplier has been on the platform (3 or more years is better), check for the Verified supplier badge, and make sure your order is covered by Trade Assurance. Order samples first.

What is Trade Assurance?

It is a protection programme on Alibaba for orders paid on the platform. It has terms and conditions, so read them. It is not a guarantee.

Is it risky to order from a supplier who is only 1 to 2 years on the platform?

It can be fine, but be more careful: make sure the supplier is Verified, your order has Trade Assurance, order samples first and start with a small order.

Why keep payment and chat on the platform?

If something goes wrong, the platform can only help with what is recorded and paid inside it. Talks about payment, quality and refunds on WhatsApp, WeChat or email are outside that protection.
