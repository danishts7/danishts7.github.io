Use AI to Pick the Right Phone: Not Every 8 GB RAM Phone Is Fast

Not every 8 GB RAM phone is fast.

By Danish · Danishts, Karachi · 22 November 2026

Two phones can both have 8 GB RAM and still feel very different. An AI tool can help you compare phones in plain language, if you ask the right questions. Here are 5 steps.

5 steps

  1. Tell an AI tool (ChatGPT, Gemini or Claude) what you use daily: social media, camera, gaming and calls. Add your budget.
  2. Ask it to compare processors: tier and generation. A higher tier and a newer generation are usually faster.
  3. Ask about the storage type (UFS is faster), the update years and the battery.
  4. Camera: 108 MP on the box does not mean great photos. Ask about sensor size, lens aperture and stabilization.
  5. AI can be outdated or wrong, so check the details before you buy.
Always confirm on the brand's official website: processor, camera, lens and updates. Models and specs change, so check the latest official details.

A simple question to start with

You can start like this: "I use social media, take many photos and play light games. My budget is X. Compare these 3 phones by processor, storage type, update years, battery and camera, and tell me what I should verify." Replace X and the models with your own.

Buy for how you use it, not for the biggest numbers. New to AI? Start with learning AI from zero.

Common questions

Can AI help me choose a phone?

Yes. Tell an AI tool what you use daily and your budget, and ask it to compare processors, storage type, updates, battery and camera. Then verify the answers.

Is every phone with 8 GB RAM fast?

No. The processor (tier and generation), storage type and software also affect speed, so two 8 GB phones can feel very different.

Does a 108 MP camera mean great photos?

Not necessarily. Ask about sensor size, lens aperture and stabilization, and check sample photos.

Can I trust what AI says about phone specs?

Not completely. AI can be outdated or wrong, so always confirm the details on the brand's official website before you buy.

What should I confirm on the official website?

The processor, the camera, the lens and the number of update years.
