Amazon Listing Keywords: A Simple Step-by-Step Plan

And what to write in each field.

By Danish · Danishts, Karachi · 22 November 2026

Good keywords help buyers find your Amazon listing. But keywords alone do not work if you ignore the fields or stuff them. This simple plan shows how to find keywords and where to put them.

6 steps to a keyword plan

  1. Shortlist 5 to 8 relevant competitors. Collect their keywords, but never use competitor brand names.
  2. Draft your listing with the best keywords.
  3. Optimize the draft with a listing optimization tool.
  4. Go live.
  5. Wait at least 2 weeks. Check impressions, clicks and sales. Change one thing at a time.
  6. No results? Test other keywords.

What to write in each field

  • Title: short and clear, about 75 characters, with the main keyword first.
  • Bullets: 5 detailed points, about 150 to 250 characters each.
  • Description: explain more, up to about 2,000 characters.
  • Backend keywords: use the space (under 250 bytes), with no repeats and no brand names.
Do not ignore any field. Do not stuff any field. Limits change, so check Seller Central for the rules of your category.

Common questions

How do I find keywords for an Amazon listing?

Shortlist 5 to 8 relevant competitors and collect their keywords, without competitor brand names. Then draft your listing with the best keywords and improve it with a listing optimization tool.

How long should an Amazon title be?

A short, clear title of about 75 characters with the main keyword first works well. Limits can change, so check Seller Central for your category.

What are backend keywords?

They are extra search terms you add in the hidden keyword field of your listing. Use the space (under 250 bytes), with no repeats and no brand names.

How long should I wait before changing my listing?

Wait at least 2 weeks and check impressions, clicks and sales. Change one thing at a time so you know what made the difference.

Can I use competitor brand names as keywords?

No. Use relevant keywords, but not competitor brand names.
