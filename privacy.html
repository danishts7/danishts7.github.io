Privacy Policy

A short and honest explanation of what this website does with your information.

1. What we collect

This website has no accounts or logins. The only place it asks for personal details is the contact form: your first name, last name, email, your message and, if you choose, your mobile number. This information is sent to us by email and is used only to reply to you and to work on your project. If you contact us on WhatsApp or by email instead, we only see what you choose to send us.

2. Your topic choice on the blog

The blog remembers which topic (IT or Amazon) you read most, so it can show you similar posts first. This is saved only in your own browser on your own device. It is not sent to us or to anyone else, and you can remove it by clearing your browser's site data.

3. Outside services

Some parts of the site are provided by other companies, which may receive technical information such as your IP address when the page loads and may use their own cookies under their own policies:

  • Google Fonts for the text fonts.
  • Facebook for the Facebook page box on some pages.
  • GitHub Pages for hosting the website.
  • Web3Forms, a form service that delivers what you write in the contact form to our email. It handles that information under its own privacy policy.

Links to WhatsApp, Facebook, LinkedIn and Fiverr take you to those sites, which have their own privacy policies.

4. Analytics and advertising

At the time of writing, this website does not use advertising cookies or visitor tracking tools. If this changes, this page will be updated.

5. Contact

For any question about privacy, message us on WhatsApp: +92 321 3434423 or email contact@danishts.com.
