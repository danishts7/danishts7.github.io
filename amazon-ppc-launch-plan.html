Amazon PPC: Why Every New Launch Needs a Plan

Budget, bid and ACoS, explained simply.

By Danish · Danishts, Karachi · 13 November 2026

A new Amazon product has no reviews and no sales history, so almost nobody finds it. Amazon PPC (pay-per-click) ads help the product get seen. But running ads without a plan can burn money fast.

What is PPC?

With PPC you pay only when someone clicks your ad. Your ads can show at the top of search results and on product pages.

3 numbers to watch

  • Budget: how much you are willing to spend per day.
  • Bid: how much you pay for one click.
  • ACoS: advertising cost of sales, which shows how much you spend on ads compared to the sales they bring.

ACoS example

If you spend $10 on ads and get $50 in sales, your ACoS is 10 ÷ 50 = 20%. Whether 20% is good depends on your profit margin. That is why margin must be calculated first.

The most common mistake

Running ads without keyword research and without a profit plan. You pay for clicks on the wrong words, and you do not know at what ACoS you start losing money.

Our approach: plan the PPC budget before you order stock. If the numbers do not work on paper, they will not work after you have paid for inventory.
