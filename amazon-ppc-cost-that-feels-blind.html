Amazon PPC: The Cost That Feels Blind

But it's the strategy that grows your sales.

By Danish · Danishts, Karachi · 17 November 2026

Most Amazon costs are easy to see and plan. PPC is different. You pay first and see results later, so it can feel like spending blind. The fix is not to avoid PPC, but to plan it. Without a plan, PPC is blind spending. With a plan, it grows your sales.

Costs you can see

  • Amazon fees
  • Storage fees
  • Product cost
  • VA fee, if you work with a virtual assistant

These are predictable, so you can plan for them before you launch.

Why PPC feels blind

With PPC, money goes out first and results come later. You pay for clicks before you know which keywords will bring sales. That is why a budget and a clear method matter more here than anywhere else.

How PPC works: 4 steps

  1. Start with an Auto campaign. Let Amazon show what buyers search.
  2. Move proven keywords to a Manual campaign. You get more control over bids and keywords.
  3. Test keywords small, spend more on winners, and cut losers after enough clicks.
  4. Be patient. Judge results after enough data, in weeks, not days.
Our approach: plan the PPC budget upfront, set a daily cap, and review regularly. If the numbers do not work on paper, they will not work after you have paid for stock.

To understand the three numbers behind this (budget, bid and ACoS), read Amazon PPC: why every new launch needs a plan. PPC is also step 7 in the 7 steps from research to launch.

Common questions

What is the difference between Auto and Manual campaigns on Amazon?

In an Auto campaign Amazon chooses where to show your ad, which helps you discover what buyers search. In a Manual campaign you choose the keywords and bids yourself, which gives you more control.

How long should I wait before judging Amazon PPC results?

Judge results after you have enough data, which usually means weeks, not days. Early numbers from a few clicks can be misleading.

Should I stop a keyword after a few clicks?

Not too early. Let a keyword collect enough clicks before you cut it, then spend more on the keywords that bring real sales.

Why should I plan the PPC budget before ordering stock?

Because ads can cost more than your profit per sale. If the numbers do not work on paper, they will not work after you have paid for inventory.

What is a daily cap in Amazon PPC?

A daily cap is the most you allow a campaign to spend in one day. It stops a campaign from spending more than you planned.
