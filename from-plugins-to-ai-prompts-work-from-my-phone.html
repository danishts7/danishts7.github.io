From Plugins and Tools to AI Prompts

Work that once needed a PC now happens from my phone.

By Danish · Danishts, Karachi · 22 November 2026

Not long ago, building and updating a website meant many plugins, many tools and a PC for every job. Today I do most of that from my phone. This is how it works for me, in simple words.

Before

  • Building a website needed many plugins and tools.
  • Every job needed a PC.

Today

  1. My website danishts.com is connected to Claude.
  2. I send a prompt. It adds the content and uploads the photos.
  3. I make an image in one AI tool, give it to another, and it goes live on my website.
  4. Most of it happens from my phone.
I use free plans only and work within their limits. That means some tasks take a little planning, but small jobs get done.
