IT Documentation for a Small Office: 3 Sheets to Keep

Small or big office, write down what matters.

By Danish · Danishts, Karachi · 22 November 2026

When something breaks in the office, the first question is usually: what did we do last time? If nobody wrote it down, you start again from zero. A few simple sheets fix this. This works for a small office and a big one.

3 sheets to keep

  • Setup Sheet: IP reservations, printers, file shares and user access to PCs.
  • PC Maintenance Log: date, PC or user, audit, scan, update, issue and fix. Log the important things, not every tiny one.
  • Asset Sheet: laptops, routers, switches and printers: date received, issues and date retired.

Why it helps

When an issue comes back, you can check what it was and how you fixed it last time. You also know what equipment you own and how old it is, which helps when you plan replacements or a backup, as in protecting your PCs and data.

Never write domain or admin passwords in the sheet. Keep them in a secure backup, such as a password manager, and give the owner or management access.

Protect the sheet too

  • Limit who can open it.
  • Keep a backup copy.

Write it down. Fix it faster. Good records also support a secure network, see 6 IT rules for an office network, and make slow-PC problems easier to trace, see slow PC? not always the internet.

Common questions

What is IT documentation for a small office?

It is a simple written record of your office setup and equipment, such as a Setup Sheet, a PC Maintenance Log and an Asset Sheet, so you can fix problems faster.

What should I write in a PC maintenance log?

Write the date, the PC or user, and what was done: audit, scan, update, the issue and the fix. Log the important things, not every tiny one.

Can I store admin passwords in the sheet?

No. Never write domain or admin passwords in the sheet. Keep them in a secure backup such as a password manager and give the owner or management access.

What goes in an IT asset sheet?

List laptops, routers, switches and printers with the date received, any issues and the date retired.

How do I keep the sheet safe?

Limit who has access to it and keep a backup copy.
